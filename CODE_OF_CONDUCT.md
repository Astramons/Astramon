# Code Of Conduct

Astramon contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Respect different skill levels and time zones.
- Keep project discussion safe for contributors and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Spam, scams, or misleading wallet instructions.
- Attempts to bypass user consent in wallet transactions.

Maintainers may remove content or block participation when behavior harms the project or its users.
